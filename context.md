# Project Context

## 1. Product Summary
Namami Gange Tours and Travel is a travel business that currently relies on manual follow-up for customer inquiries received through calls and WhatsApp. The company loses business because there is no structured system to capture, prioritize, and track each lead from first enquiry to finalized booking.

This project is an MVP for a travel enquiry management system that helps the business record inbound customer requests, track their status, send follow-up reminders, generate branded quote/itinerary content, and maintain post-booking documents for the customer and vendors.

## 2. Problem Statement
The business receives multiple travel inquiries every day, but there is no centralized system for storing those leads or monitoring progress. Queries are captured inconsistently, follow-ups become manual, and important customer details are often missing or delayed. As a result, the company loses leads and misses repeat business and referral opportunities.

## 3. Business Opportunity
The company wants a lightweight but scalable enquiry-to-booking workflow that captures customer intent and reduces the effort involved in manual tracking. The solution should give agents a cleaner way to manage leads, respond faster, and create branded travel proposals without switching across multiple disconnected tools.

## 4. Goals
- Record and track at least 90% of incoming enquiries from calls and WhatsApp.
- Increase the quality and consistency of follow-ups.
- Ensure all customer enquiries have complete required data before follow-up.
- Convert more qualified leads into confirmed bookings.
- Create a standard workflow from enquiry capture to quote generation and post-booking document handling.

## 5. Non-goals
- This project is not a full AI chatbot or AI calling agent.
- It is not a giant travel ERP or enterprise suite.
- It does not aim to replace the existing premium positioning or broader business operations system in one step.
- It is not meant to solve long-duration, low-priority customer journeys in the first release.

## 6. Target Users
### Travel sales executives
- Receive leads from WhatsApp, calls, and other channels.
- Need quick access to customer details and follow-up status.
- Need reminders to convert enquiries into bookings.

### Operations staff
- Need access to vouchers, traveller details, and booking-related documentation.
- Need to coordinate with hotels, customers, and internal teams.

### Business owner / manager
- Needs visibility into enquiry volume, conversion, and follow-up health.
- Wants to track business performance without relying on manual spreadsheets.

## 7. Success Metrics
- 90% of demand enquiries captured and recorded.
- Enquiries recorded with complete information in most cases.
- Lower rate of incomplete or inaccurate customer data.
- Increased conversion of enquiries into confirmed bookings.
- Reduced manual admin effort and better lead follow-up speed.

## 8. Key Product Flows
### Enquiry Capture
- A customer inquiry arrives via call or WhatsApp.
- Essential information is stored in a standardized enquiry record.
- The system captures a structured summary of destination, dates, travellers, budget, and notes.

### Follow-up Workflow
- The enquiry record is assigned to an agent.
- The business can track whether the customer was contacted, responded, or needs another follow-up.
- Agents can move enquiry status through stages such as new, contacted, quoted, booked, follow-up, closed.

### Quote / Itinerary Creation
- After review, an itinerary or quote is created for the client.
- The final output is sent to the customer only after human verification.

### Post-booking Operations
- Vouchers and relevant documents are generated and sent to the customer and hotels.
- Optional payment, traveller document, and trip operations data can be managed after booking.

## 9. Functional Requirements
- Capture enquiry details automatically from WhatsApp and prefill fields where possible.
- Maintain a centralized customer enquiry dashboard.
- Support structured follow-up management.
- Generate branded travel proposals and itinerary outputs.
- Send vouchers and documents to relevant stakeholders.
- Allow updates when customer plans or dates change.

## 10. Non-functional Requirements
- Performance: enquiry data should be captured within 10 minutes of customer interaction.
- Quality: itineraries and vouchers should match company brand and template standards.
- Reliability: date and destination changes should be updated accurately.
- Security: customer records must be protected against unauthorized access.
- Transparency: all captured data should be traceable.
- Usability: at least 80% of required details should be captured within the first two interactions.

## 11. MVP Scope for Portfolio Project
The portfolio version should focus on the highest-value parts of the workflow without trying to build the complete enterprise system.

Recommended MVP includes:
- Lead dashboard
- Enquiry form and storage
- Follow-up and status tracking
- Quote / itinerary generation mock or template flow
- Document and voucher records
- Basic authentication and role access
- Reporting summary for conversion and lead volume
